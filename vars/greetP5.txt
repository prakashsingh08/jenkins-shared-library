<p>
    <strong>Phase 5 version.</strong> Greets one or more people. The greeting itself is produced by
    <code>com.learning.phase05.Greeter</code>, a class in <code>src/</code>; this step only reads
    configuration, validates it and delegates.
</p>

<h3>Usage</h3>
<pre>
greetP5(name: 'Prakash')
greetP5(names: ['Prakash', 'Asha', 'Ravi'])
greetP5(name: 'Prakash', greeting: 'Namaste')
</pre>

<h3>Configuration</h3>
<table>
    <tr><th>Key</th><th>Type</th><th>Required</th><th>Default</th></tr>
    <tr><td><code>name</code></td><td>String</td><td>one of <code>name</code> / <code>names</code></td><td>—</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>names</code></td><td>List</td><td>one of <code>name</code> / <code>names</code></td><td>—</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>greeting</code></td><td>String</td><td>no</td><td><code>Hello</code></td></tr>
</table>

<h3>Notes</h3>
<ul>
    <li>A class in <code>src/</code> has no connection to the pipeline, so it receives the pipeline
        as a constructor argument (<code>new Greeter(this)</code>) and calls steps through it:
        <code>script.echo</code>.</li>
</ul>
